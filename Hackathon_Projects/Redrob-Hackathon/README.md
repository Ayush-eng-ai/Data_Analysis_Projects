# Alpha Decoder - Intelligent Candidate Discovery & Ranking

## Overview

Alpha Decoder is an AI-powered candidate ranking system developed for the Redrob Intelligent Candidate Discovery & Ranking Challenge.

The system processes 100,000 candidate profiles and identifies the most relevant AI/ML candidates using experience, skills, career evidence, and behavioral hiring signals.

## Problem Statement

Develop an intelligent candidate discovery and ranking system capable of identifying the most relevant candidates for an AI/ML hiring requirement from a dataset of 100,000 candidate profiles.

## Dataset

* Total Candidates: 100,000
* Candidate Profiles: JSONL Format
* Behavioral Signals Included
* Career History Included

## Methodology

### Candidate Evaluation Signals

* Title Relevance
* Experience Match
* AI/ML Skills
* Career Evidence
* Behavioral Signals
* Hiring Readiness

### Ranking Formula

Final Score =
Experience Score +
Title Score +
Career Evidence Score +
AI Skill Score +
Behavior Score -
Penalty Score
